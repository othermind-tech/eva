# Conversation History

Every conversation you have with EVA is saved automatically and remains accessible indefinitely. Conversations do not expire, and nothing is deleted unless you choose to delete it.

---

## Finding past conversations

The conversation list organises your history by recency — today, yesterday, the past week, and older. A search bar filters by title as you type. To narrow further, a filter option lets you restrict results by last activity (past 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, or 30 days) and sort by recency, alphabetical order, or creation date.

Conversations linked to a project appear under that project rather than in the main list. To find them, navigate to the project and open its Conversations tab.

---

## Exporting

Any conversation can be exported as a file. Three formats are available:

**JSON** — the complete structured record: every message, timestamp, tool call input and result, and execution metadata. Use this for archiving, feeding into other tools programmatically, or when you want a full audit trail of what EVA did and how.

**Markdown** — a clean, readable version of the conversation with formatting intact. Good for sharing with others, dropping into a knowledge base, or publishing as a document.

**Text** — plain prose with no markup. Useful when you need only the words, without any structure.

---

## Organising

**Renaming** — EVA generates a title from the content of your first message. This can be changed at any time. Giving conversations clear names makes them easier to find later, especially in long-running work where many threads exist.

**Moving to a project** — A standalone conversation can be linked to a project at any time. Once linked, the conversation gains access to the project's context and appears under the project's Conversations tab. This is useful when a standalone exploration grows into structured ongoing work.

---

## Deleting

Conversations can be deleted individually from the conversation list or from within the conversation itself. Deletion is permanent — the conversation is removed from the database immediately and cannot be recovered.

If there is any chance you will want to refer back to a conversation, export it before deleting.
